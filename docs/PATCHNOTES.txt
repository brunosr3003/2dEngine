Nov 13, 2026 — Fixes

- Gear: the level to use a piece is now its item level (a level 21 piece needs level 21). Crafting recipes make items of the recipe level.
- Dungeons: auto play now goes to the monsters of the current room instead of walking toward ones behind a closed gate.

Nov 12, 2026 — Big economy update: buy orders, rarer resources and keys

- New in the Market: Buy orders. Post what you want to buy (item, amount and price each); the gold is held and other players can sell to your order. Cancel any time to get the rest of the gold back.
- Craft resources are rarer and worth trading: Darksteel now comes only from rocks (plus daily rewards and the shop), and monsters drop craft materials less often. Rocks give the same as before.
- Craft keys: purple is now level 40-69 and legendary 70+. A ready legendary key is very rare luck; the main road is combining.
- Combine (keys, pets and mounts): every 25th attempt of a color is guaranteed. The counter is shown in the Combine tab and a lucky success does not reset it.
- Field boss chests no longer give Darksteel: they can give a craft key of the area's color instead (never legendary).
- Dungeon chests no longer give Darksteel.
- Monsters far below your level give less XP (more than 5 levels below: -8% per level, at least 20%). Drops are not affected.
- Caverns are gone: dungeons are the Porão ones, which cost a key and have no daily limit.
- Dungeons: if your connection drops, you have 90 seconds to reconnect and get back into the same run.
- New in the Bag: Help tab. See your attack, defense and health against what is expected at your level, which pieces hold you back, and where to hunt and which dungeon to run.
- Server list: shows how full each server is (green, yellow, red) instead of the player count.
- New icon for the Key Summon Scroll.
- New Combine animation: your items gather into an orb and reveal the result (tap to skip).
- The ping shown in game is now your real connection ping (it used to add the server tick and a frame, so it showed higher than it was).
- The game now measures connection quality (ping and frame rate) so we can find and fix lag faster.
- Fixed: after a dungeon you could come back as another character of your account.
- Character names that differ only in capital letters are no longer allowed.
