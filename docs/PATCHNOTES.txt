Nov 13, 2026 — Clans, PK modes and the Prime Merchant

- The Path tree is now a huge star-map web: 180 nodes across four arms, with notables, keystones that trade a big bonus for a cost, and bridges between paths for hybrid builds. It takes about 490 points to complete.
- New Path tree screen: drag and pinch to explore, every node has its own icon, and learned nodes light up the constellation.
- The Path tree was reset for free: all your path points are back to spend again.
- Resetting the Path tree is paid in gold only.
- With a rebirth aura, gear auras no longer cover your body: the armour and accessory glows give way to it, and your weapon keeps a lighter glow.
- Rebirth auras were redesigned: each tier has its own look (leaves, frost, runes, a golden halo and a black hole), and the rebirth celebration matches it.
- New: the open sea. At the dock of the Grove, the Glacier, the Waste or the Plateau, ask the Harbour Captain to "Sail" and take your own ship out between the islands. Sail up to an island's dock to go ashore.
- Your first ship, the Sloop, comes from the story quest that first takes you to the Glacier. Players already past it find a Sloop in their Mail.
- At sea: gather floating wreckage (wood, Steel, Darksteel and Energy), open drifting chests, and keep clear of the sharks and eels that gnaw at your hull. If the hull breaks you wash up at the dock, and the Captain repairs the ship for copper. PK modes apply at sea, except close to the docks.
- The Harbour Captain's trips between islands now cost copper, shown on the button. The story's own trips stay free.
- New: the Shipwright. At the Harbour Captain, build bigger ships from your current one: the Schooner, the Galleon and the Man-o'-War, each with a tougher hull and a little more speed.
- Ship upgrades: put up to 5 stars each on your ship's Hull, Sails and Cannons. Upgrades always succeed and stay with the ship when you trade or sell it.
- New sea material: Wreck Planks, found in wreckage, drifting chests and sea monsters. Each sea route now has a dangerous elite monster.
- New ship skills: at sea your skill bar becomes your ship's, with Broadside, Bow Cannon, Emergency Repair and Smoke. The cannons can go on AUTO.
- New in the TP shop: sail colours and figureheads to dress up your ship.
- New: the Colossus Chest. Every world boss now drops one for the player who lands the killing blow, and it rides on your ship's deck. Open it on land for a boss-sized prize, or sail it to other islands first: each new island you dock at raises the payout, up to x3.
- Treasure at sea comes with a risk: while you carry a Colossus Chest your ship flies a skull flag and anyone can attack you at sea, even in Peaceful mode. The waters right by the docks stay safe.
- Sink a treasure ship and its chest floats at the wreck for anyone to haul aboard; defeat its captain yourself and the chest goes straight to your deck. Ships are never lost, and treasure is never destroyed.
- New: lawless waters on three routes away from the Grove, marked in red on the map. Anyone can attack anyone there, and no one gets PK points for it.
- Attacking an innocent Peaceful ship still earns PK points, just like on land.
- The Corsair path has new sea nodes: more cannon damage, a tougher hull, faster treasure hauling, a bigger Colossus Chest payout, and the Pirate's Code keystone (more cannon damage against treasure ships, but you are always a target at sea).
- Flash offers now include TP (at least 400, never less than the same money buys in the TP shop) on top of their bonus, and the Offers tab got a new look.
- The Teleport Scroll is back: the village Alchemist sells it for copper, and on a long trip (map, a quest's Go, an NPC) the "Teleport" button jumps you to that spot on the same island. Other islands are still reached by boat from the Harbour Captain.
- The first story quests give Teleport Scrolls, and when you run out the button says so, with a "Find" that shows where to buy more. The button sits above the travel strip, clear of the chat.
- Health regeneration: 0.5 HP/s in combat and 2 HP/s out of combat, plus your Vitality bonus. Regenerating and drinking potions now show a visual effect.
- Bag: a new "Equip best" button puts on your strongest pieces in one tap, keeping your weapon and armour type.
- Tougher monsters give more XP: wolves, bears, owlbears and other high-health beasts now pay for their extra health. Auto combat targets the nearest enemy by default.
- Items left in the My Island chest were sent to your Mail.
- The Prime Merchant now sells dungeon keys for gold (3 of each a day), and "Find" shows the Prime Merchant for everything it sells.
- Story dungeon quests on each island now also give 3 free Key Summon scrolls.
- At the level cap, a "Rebirth available" badge stays on screen (even in dungeons and boss fights); tap it to open your character sheet.
- New in every town square: the Prime Merchant. Buy XP Potions, Energy, Darksteel, craft keys (up to purple) and craft materials (up to blue) for gold, at the market price plus 15%, with a daily limit per item. The board shows the price trend.
- XP Potions now come from daily quests (up to 3 a day) and the Prime Merchant, no longer from story, side or one-off quests.
- Clans: set a [TAG] of 2 to 5 letters, shown on names, in chat and in your party. See which members are online on any channel and island, and when the others were last seen. New Officer rank: invites, removes members and edits the clan notice. The clan window was redone.
- PK modes: Free, Party, Clan and Peaceful. Players with PK on or a red name can be attacked by anyone. PK points only come from killing a clean Peaceful player; they go down by 1 per hour online or can be forgiven for gold. Dying with PK points costs more XP.
- On the Magic Island, outside the arrival port, PK is locked to Party mode and gives no PK points.
- PvP balance: skills and basic attacks deal much less damage to players, and fighting far below your level is penalised.
- Twin Pistols hit harder and dodge more in the early levels; the bonus fades as you level and is gone by level 50.
- Market: type the amount in every number field, gear tier shown in item names, scroll the lists with your finger. Listings far from the market value are marked in red, with Adjust price / Adjust all.
- New level scale, 1 to 100: the Grove is levels 1-30, the Glacier 30-50, the Waste 50-70 and the Plateau 70-100; everything beyond is the "100+" band, opened by your Power. Monsters keep their strength: only the numbers changed, and your character keeps all its progress.
- Boss and dungeon chests also give Energy, and Energy veins and rocks give more Energy and Darksteel the higher the level of the place.
- Killing monsters below your level costs less XP: full XP up to 8 levels below, then -5% per level, never under 40%. The Help tab's hunting range follows it.
- Field bosses no longer reset so easily: they chase longer, go farther before giving up, and only recover 20% of their health when they walk home.
- Leveling up has a bigger celebration with its own fanfare, and new sounds for quests, chests, Energy, refining, crafting and the mail.
- "Where to get" now lists the chests of field bosses, Grutas and Porões for Darksteel, Energy, materials, keys and gear.
- Fixed: the "Where to get" button on an item card sat on top of the close button.
- On your first login after this update, a message shows your old and new level.
- First-win dungeon rewards go straight to your bag; only what does not fit goes to the Mail, and you are told.
- The Waste has many more monsters, and the Waste and the Plateau have new quests and contracts at the Quest Master.
- The Magic Island steps now open by Power instead of level.
- My Island has been removed, along with its quests. If you were on one of them, your story simply continues from the next step.
- The level cap is now 100. Everything above 100 is the "100+" band, opened by your Power instead of your level.
- Dungeon Power requirements now follow how strong real players are at each level: the first stage still lets in anyone of the level, the top stages ask for good gear.
- No level requirement to craft any recipe or to travel between islands: what matters is your Power and your materials.
- PvP is balanced by Power instead of level: a much stronger player cannot start a fight with one under half their Power (unless that player chose a PK mode or has a red name), and the weaker side loses damage against a far stronger one.
- Dungeons and bosses give much more XP: every kill inside a Gruta or Porão gives double XP, and bosses give twice what they used to.
- Chests of field bosses, Grutas and Porões give Darksteel again and better gear: green pieces at 50%, blue from level 38 content, purple (very rare) from level 64; a better chest gives a lot more Darksteel and materials.
- Leveling is faster from level 20 on: the XP slowdown added on Nov 12 was eased (from level 50 a level now takes over a third less time than before this update).
- Weekend event every week: XP x1.5 and drops x1.5 from Saturday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 (Brasília time, UTC-3).
- Market lots: sell an amount for a total price, so cheap items can cost less than 1 gold each (1,000 Copper for 3 gold). Buyers take the whole lot or part of it, always paying whole gold; the screen shows how much the same gold buys. Buy orders work the same way.
- Bag: View on market, Sell and Buy order shortcuts on items.
- Trade redone: start it from Menu > Direct trade, every tradable item of your bag (Darksteel and Copper too) plus gold and Energy, typed amounts and item icons. Any change unlocks both sides before confirming.
- Ward Charm protects refines up to +7.
- Monsters no longer spawn right next to purple rock veins.
- Skills follow the weapon's base speed: attack speed now speeds up the basic attack only. Life Drain heals from the damage that lands.
- Story: every Porão the story sends you to (Smuggler's Cellar, Thunder Vault, Seraph Reliquary, Robot Foundry, Sunken Galleon) now comes after steps to gather its key materials and craft its key, like the Shipwreck Cellar. The material steps only ask you to HAVE the wood and Steel in your bag: if you already carry them, they pass at once, nothing is taken, and the step pays the same amount back.
- The early wood steps no longer get stuck among monsters: the grove next to the Grove's town is kept free of monsters, and the auto quest sends tree-felling quests there first.
- New bonus packs, for a few cents and a limited time: if the Shipwreck Cellar beats you (you fall, leave or run out of time), a Starter Pack pops up with 400 TP, 10,000 gold, 10,000 Energy and 10,000 Darksteel for 0.99. Later falls can offer your own weapon in Blue at your level (with gold), and then a full Blue set with TP, each showing how much Power you would gain.
- New level packs at levels 20, 50, 70 and 100. Every pack gives at least the TP its price buys in the shop, plus a bonus (gold, Energy, Darksteel, summon scrolls or gear). Each pack appears once per account.
- Gear bought in a pack goes straight to your bag (to the Mail if the bag is full); its gold goes to your purse.
- REBIRTH: at level 100, open the Character Sheet and tap Rebirth. You go back to level 1 and your attribute points are reset (their Energy is refunded); you keep your items and gear, gold, TP, pets, mounts, proficiencies, story and skill tiers.
- Every rebirth gives +5% to every attribute for good (stacking, no cap), Rebirth Essence (more on higher rebirths) and a rebirth chest with Darksteel, a crafting key and crafting materials in the rebirth's color. Leveling gets a little slower with each rebirth up to the 15th, then much slower with every rebirth after that, with no cap. After your first rebirth, quests no longer ask for a level: every level lock counts as met.
- Rebirth milestones: a body aura that changes color (green at 1, blue at 3, purple at 5, gold at 10, a black hole at 15, brighter every 5 from 20), +10 bag slots at the 2nd and +10 bank slots at the 7th. Everyone online is told when a player reaches a milestone.
- Rebirths show as "R3" before the name, in the Character Sheet, and the Level ranking is ordered by rebirths first, then level. Each rebirth has its own celebration, bigger at every milestone.
- PATH TREE: from your first rebirth on, every 10 levels give a path point (10 per climb, and they stay across rebirths). Open Character Sheet > PATHS to learn nodes in four paths of 15: Assassin (crit, attack speed, dexterity, movement), Mage (wisdom, mana, attack, mana regen), Guardian (defense, health, damage reduction, regen, poise) and Corsair (drops, gold, ore, Energy, Darksteel, better loot). Mix paths freely; each path's nodes are learned in order.
- PATH ACTIVES: nodes 5, 10 and 15 of each path also teach an active skill, used from new skill slots 4, 5 and 6 (opened at rebirths 1, 3 and 5; keys 4-6 on PC). Assassin: Shadowstep, Invisibility and Lethal Fury (more critical chance and critical damage for a few seconds). Mage: Arcane Nova, Meteor and Time Warp. Guardian: Taunt, Bulwark Shield and Unbreakable. Corsair: Treasure Sense, Plunder and Golden Fleet.
- A newly learned active goes to the next free slot; choose which active sits in each slot from the path tree. Tap to use, drag up for AUTO, like your weapon skills.
- Invisibility: monsters lose track of you and players cannot target you for a few seconds, until you attack.
- From rebirth 10, each path's final active becomes Supreme and is 50% stronger.
- Reset all your path points at any time for 1,000 gold per point spent, or for 20 Rebirth Essence.
- GEAR ASCENSION: at the Forge, the new Ascend tab adds stars to a gear piece (weapon, offhand, armor and accessories; not pets or mounts), up to 5. Each star gives +8% of the piece's base stats (+40% at 5 stars), on top of the refine. Ascending never fails and costs Rebirth Essence (10, 20, 40, 80, 160) plus Darksteel (2,000 doubling up to 32,000). Stars stay with the piece in trades, the market, the bank and the mail, and upgrading two pieces keeps the higher star count.
- ESSENCE SHOP: open Character Sheet > ESSENCE (from your first rebirth on) to spend Rebirth Essence on a path reset, legendary craft keys (Scale, Claw, Horn, Leather), Ward Charms and cosmetics (Crown, Knight outfit, Void Ring skin, Spectral Coat mount skin). Purchases go to your bag, or to the Mail if it is full.

Nov 12, 2026 — Big economy update: buy orders, rarer resources and keys

- Leveling takes longer from level 20 on: from level 50, each level asks about twice the time it used to.
- Gear has no level requirement to equip any more. Crafting recipes make items of the recipe level.
- Drops: purple gear is now very rare from chests and legendary gear no longer drops at all; craft is the way to the top colors. Field-boss chests follow the area level.
- Magic Island: the entry pass is sold only in the TP shop and can no longer be traded or sold on the Market. The XP islet gives x1.5.
- Fixed: story steps that asked for a Cavern now accept any dungeon.
- Dungeons: auto play no longer walks back to the entrance wall after the first group; it always heads to the monsters of the current room.
- New in the Market: Buy orders. Post what you want to buy (item, amount and price each); the gold is held and other players can sell to your order. Cancel any time to get the rest of the gold back.
- Craft resources are rarer and worth trading: Darksteel now comes only from rocks (plus daily rewards and the shop), and monsters drop craft materials less often. Rocks give the same as before.
- Craft keys: purple is now level 40-69 and legendary 70+. A ready legendary key is very rare luck; the main road is combining.
- Combine (keys, pets and mounts): every 25th attempt of a color is guaranteed. The counter is shown in the Combine tab and a lucky success does not reset it.
- Field boss chests no longer give Darksteel: they can give a craft key of the area's color instead (never legendary).
- Dungeon chests no longer give Darksteel.
- Monsters far below your level give less XP (more than 5 levels below: -8% per level, at least 20%). Drops are not affected.
- Caverns are gone: dungeons are the Porão ones, which cost a key and have no daily limit.
- Dungeons: if your connection drops, you have 90 seconds to reconnect and get back into the same run.
- New in the Bag: Help tab. See your attack, defense and health against what is expected at your level, which pieces hold you back, and where to hunt and which dungeon to run.
- Server list: shows how full each server is (green, yellow, red) instead of the player count.
- New icon for the Key Summon Scroll.
- New Combine animation: your items gather into an orb and reveal the result (tap to skip).
- The ping shown in game is now your real connection ping (it used to add the server tick and a frame, so it showed higher than it was).
- The game now measures connection quality (ping and frame rate) so we can find and fix lag faster.
- Fixed: after a dungeon you could come back as another character of your account.
- Character names that differ only in capital letters are no longer allowed.
